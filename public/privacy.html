Chorely Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Chorely helps households and teams organize chores. This policy describes the information used to provide the service.

Information we collect

We collect the email address and authentication information needed to create and secure your account. We also store content you add to Chorely, such as chores, schedules, team-member details, availability, and completion history.

How information is used

We use this information only to authenticate you, operate Chorely, synchronize your content, and maintain the security and reliability of the service. Chorely does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers

Chorely uses Supabase for authentication and hosted data storage. If you choose to connect Google Calendar, information needed for that feature is sent to Google at your direction and is subject to Google's policies.

Retention and deletion

We retain account information while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account from Account settings inside Chorely. Account deletion removes your sign-in and associated profile information, except information we must retain for legal or security reasons.

Your choices

You can choose whether to enable notifications or connect Google Calendar. You may disconnect Google Calendar through Chorely and your Google Account settings.

Children

Chorely is intended to be managed by a parent, guardian, or other adult and is not directed to children under 13 for independent account creation.
